Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who processes your data

Yiji is self-hosted software. Each person runs their own copy on their own computer and is the only one who controls the data it handles. No Yiji server receives your banking data.

What data is used

  • Account identifiers and transactions (amount, currency, dates, status) from the accounts you choose to connect.
  • A session identifier and its expiry date, used to fetch transactions again without asking you to log in each time.

Why

Only to show your own spending in charts. Data is not sold, shared, profiled or used for advertising.

Where it is stored

On the computer running Yiji, in its local data folder. Bank access goes through Enable Banking, a licensed account information service provider, under their own privacy policy.

How long

Access lasts at most 90 days, or less if you revoke it. Delete the local data folder to remove all stored data immediately.

Your rights

You can revoke access at any time in your bank or by deleting Yiji’s data. Because the data never leaves your computer, there is nothing to request from us. For questions, open an issue on the site repository.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies or analytics. The callback page passes the bank’s response directly to your local Yiji and stores nothing.
