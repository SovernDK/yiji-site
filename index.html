Yiji

A self-hosted personal finance app. It reads your bank transactions through Enable Banking and shows where your money goes, on your own computer.

How it works

  1. You run Yiji locally and choose “Connect bank”.
  2. Your bank asks you to approve read-only access.
  3. The bank returns you to this site, which hands you straight back to your local Yiji.
